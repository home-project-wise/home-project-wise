How the calculator works

It uses widely shared design rules of thumb, the same ones explained in our guide to choosing the right rug size:

  • Living room: the rug extends about 8 to 12 inches past the furniture on every side. Measure all legs on, or front legs on the rug, as shown in the form.
  • Dining room: the rug extends 24 to 30 inches past the table on every side, so the chairs stay on the rug when pulled out.
  • Bedroom: the rug sits under the lower two-thirds of the bed and extends 18 to 24 inches past the sides and the foot.
  • Border: the bare floor you choose is subtracted from the room to find the largest rug that fits.

The results are estimates. Rugs are sold in standard sizes that vary by seller, and sources differ by a few inches, so tape the size on the floor and live with it for a day before you order.

Frequently Asked Questions

What size rug do I need for a living room?

At least the front legs of the sofa and chairs should sit on the rug, or all legs in a larger room. In many rooms that means 8 x 10 ft, and 9 x 12 ft in larger ones. Use the calculator for your own measurements, then tape the size on the floor.

How much bare floor should I leave around a rug?

About 12 to 18 inches (30 to 45 cm) in a living room is a common target, although sources range from 8 to 24 inches. In a narrow room, accept less rather than shrinking the rug until it floats.

What size rug goes under a dining table?

Add at least 24 inches (60 cm) to every side of the table so the chairs stay on the rug when pulled out. Some guides recommend up to 30 inches (75 cm).

Does the calculator work in centimetres?

Yes. Choose Centimetres at the top and the calculator uses common metric rug sizes such as 160 x 230 cm and 200 x 290 cm.
