Picture Hanging Planner

Use your own measurements to plan art, measured hook positions, confirmed stud locations and gallery walls. This is a placement worksheet only; it does not choose fixings or assess load capacity.

Your measurements

Units

One piece on an open wall

57 in (145 cm) is an editable gallery guideline, not a rule. Measure the actual hardware bearing point from the frame top.

Above furniture

6–10 in (15–25 cm) is a design range only. Choose and measure your own gap.

Wide piece with two measured hooks

Measure each support independently. x is from the fixed wall reference edge; y is above the finished floor. No load-sharing inference is made.

Confirmed stud positions

Physically confirm each stud. 16 in and 24 in are common in some US framing, not guaranteed. Spacing never confirms a stud by itself.

Gallery wall

Enter each row in your chosen order. Row height is the tallest frame in that row; vertical gaps are between row bounds. The planner lines up the frames in a row along their bottom edges and centres each row under the whole composition: that is a layout convention, not a rule, so move frames by hand if you prefer another alignment.

One row per line; separate frames with commas. Replace this syntax example with your measurements.

A 2–3 in (5–7.6 cm) gap is a layout suggestion only; choose your own measured gap.

Worked placement examples

These are semantic examples of the same calculations used by the planner. They are not fixing or load checks.

Open wall

36 in frame; centre 57 in; measured hook drop 3 in. Hook = 57 + 36 ÷ 2 − 3 = 72 in (182.88 cm).

Above furniture

Furniture top 30 in; chosen gap 8 in; frame 24 in; drop 2 in. Bottom 38 in; top 62 in; centre 50 in; hook 60 in (152.4 cm).

Two-row gallery

Row 1: 18 × 24 and 12 × 18 in; row 2: 16 × 20 and 14 × 16 in; horizontal gap 2.5 in; vertical gap 3 in. Row heights 24 and 20 in; total height: 47 in (119.38 cm).

Before you drill

  • Check for electrical cables and pipes with a suitable detector before drilling. A stud finder is not a guarantee.
  • Confirm wall material, condition, fixing instructions and installation method independently.
  • This planner does not assess anchors, screws, studs, plaster, masonry, load sharing or structural safety.
  • Do not use for ceilings, overhead loads, stairwells, cribs or child-safety installations, heavy or valuable pieces, damaged walls or uncertain substrates. Follow manufacturer instructions or ask a qualified installer.
