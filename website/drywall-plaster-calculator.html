Drywall and Plaster Quantity Calculator

Measure the wall or ceiling surfaces you plan to cover. Add the actual board dimensions or the named plaster product's published coverage only if you have them. No standard panel size, product coverage or extra allowance is assumed.

Measure your surfaces

Enter each wall as its own rectangle. Choose a length and area unit when entering manual wall dimensions, openings, ceiling area or board dimensions. If you use only the shared-room handoff, its area is already in m² and you do not need to choose a second unit. Shared-room openings are already deducted.

Optional drywall board estimate

Enter the actual face width and length printed for your selected board. This is an area estimate only, not a cut or sheet-layout plan.

Optional plaster product estimate

Use the exact named product's published coverage per bag at its stated reference thickness. Enter the application thickness you plan to use; the tool does not assess suitability or recommend a thickness.

A bag estimate appears only when all product and thickness details are entered. The entered product coverage is not independently verified.

Formula and limits

Net surface area = measured wall rectangles − openings entered + shared-room wall area + ceiling area entered. Each manually measured wall section is length × height. Shared-room dimensions are converted to area and openings are deducted once.

Drywall boards = net area ÷ actual board face area, rounded up to whole boards. Plaster bags = net area ÷ published coverage per bag, rounded up, and are shown only when the visitor-entered application thickness matches the thickness stated with that coverage. If the thicknesses differ, no bag count is estimated; no scaling assumption is made.

This is not a sheet-layout plan or installation guide. It does not determine board orientation, framing, joints, cut reuse, plaster suitability, substrate condition, moisture performance, fire or sound ratings, structural performance or code compliance. No waste, labour, prices or product recommendations are included.
