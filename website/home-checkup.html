Home Check-up

Answer ten short statements about your home and get your top three priorities, with the guides and tools to start with.

How it works

Ten short statements cover four areas of the home: storage, design, repairs and moisture, and smart home. Each answer scores 2 (often), 1 (sometimes) or 0 (rarely). The questions that score highest become your top priorities, each with the guides and tools that help, and the bars show which area needs the most attention overall.

It is a quick self-check built from the problems our guides solve, not a home inspection. Nothing you tick is stored or sent anywhere: the result is calculated in your browser, and the link you can copy only contains your answers.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does the Home Check-up take?

About a minute: ten statements with three answers each.

Is my information stored?

No. The answers stay in your browser. The result link only encodes your answers so you can come back to it or share it.

Does the quiz replace a professional inspection?

No. It points you to practical guides. For electrical, gas, structural or serious moisture and mold problems, ask a qualified professional.
