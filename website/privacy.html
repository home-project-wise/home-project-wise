PRIVACY

Privacy

We aim to collect as little personal information as practical. This page explains what the site does and which outside services are involved.

Last updated: October 17, 2026

Analytics

Site analytics are provided by GoatCounter using its privacy-focused script. It counts page views and gives us general information such as the page visited, referring site, browser, screen size and country. It does not use advertising profiles or track you across other sites, and we do not set our own cookies.

Newsletter

If you subscribe, you give us your email address through a form run by Brevo, our newsletter service. We use it only to send the newsletter you asked for. You can unsubscribe at any time with the link in every email. The form may set technical cookies needed to process your request.

Third-party content on our pages

To display the site, your browser may load fonts from Google Fonts and some images from other servers, such as Unsplash. Those services can see your IP address and basic browser information when they deliver the files. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs. We plan to host fonts and images ourselves to reduce this, and we will update this page when that happens.

Cookies

HomeProjectWise does not set cookies of its own and does not use advertising cookies. Third-party services listed above may use their own technical cookies.

Your choices and rights

If you are in the UK, the EU or another region with data-protection rights, you can ask us what personal data we hold about you (in practice, a newsletter email address), ask us to correct or delete it, or withdraw consent to the newsletter. Use the unsubscribe link in any email, or contact us through the Contact page.

Children

The site is written for adults and is not directed at children.

Changes

We will update this page when our practices change, and the date at the top shows the latest revision.
