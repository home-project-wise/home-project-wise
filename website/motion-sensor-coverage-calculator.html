Motion Sensor Coverage Calculator

Enter the area you want to cover and the coverage area printed in the specification for your exact sensor. The result is a minimum count by area only — it does not predict where a sensor will detect movement.

Enter the two area values

Use only a coverage area explicitly printed in the manufacturer's specification for the exact sensor and the mounting height you plan to use. Do not convert detection distance and angle into an area. If the manufacturer does not state an area, there is not enough information for this calculation.

What this result means — and does not mean

Minimum count by area only = area to cover ÷ manufacturer-stated coverage area per sensor, rounded up.

The two area values must use the same unit. The calculator does not estimate coverage from detection distance, angle, mounting height or room geometry. It does not account for overlap.

Important: This is not a placement plan, a security assessment or a safety assessment. Actual coverage depends on mounting height, field of view, obstacles and sensor type. Follow the exact manufacturer's instructions and test the real route before fixing a sensor permanently. Do not rely on this count as proof that an area is monitored.
