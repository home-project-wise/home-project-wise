Bulb Brightness Check

Enter the light output printed on your bulb box and count how many bulbs and fixtures you have. This adds up your own lumens; it does not decide whether a room is bright enough.

Your room and fixtures

Only room types named in the ENERGY STAR brightness examples are listed.

This entry assumes each fixture has the same number of bulbs and each bulb has the same lumen rating. If the setup or rating differs, calculate that group separately.

Optional floor area

You can leave this blank. If you opened this tool from the shared-room section, its measured floor area is copied here and remains editable.

How to read this result

ENERGY STAR's one-page fixture sheet gives brightness examples for kitchens, bathrooms, home offices, living rooms and bedrooms. It does not set a pass/fail target for your room.

Bulb lumens describe light-source output. Lux and foot-candles describe illuminance on a surface. Fixture distribution, placement and light loss affect the light that reaches a surface, so this tool does not convert your lumens into lux or give an area-based lighting target.

Source: ENERGY STAR / EPA — Fixtures Made Easy (PDF, page 1). The PDF does not state a publication or revision date.

For practical placement, layers and glare, read Better Evening Home Lighting Without Overcomplicating It.
