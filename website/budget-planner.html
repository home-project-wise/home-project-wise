How the planner works

Pick a project, type the prices you see in your own shops, and the planner adds them up by priority. Must have items solve the problem, nice to have items make it better, and later items are the purchases our guides tell you to wait on. If you set a budget, the plan shows what fits and the order to buy things in, so the essentials are never the part you cannot afford.

The item lists come from the matching guides. We do not supply prices: they vary by country, shop and week, and a stale number would mislead you. Nothing you type is sent anywhere. The link you can copy contains your list so you can reopen or share it.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does the planner know what things cost?

No. You enter the prices you find, so the totals are always based on your own shops and your own country.

What do the three priorities mean?

Must have: needed to fix the problem. Nice to have: improves the result. Later or only if needed: purchases to delay until the basics are in place and you know you need them.

Can I save my plan?

Yes. Use Copy a link to this plan. The link contains your list, so opening it later restores everything. Nothing is stored on our servers.
