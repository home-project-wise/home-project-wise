Room Lighting Planner

Choose one published source, then choose a room or task that appears in its table. Enter the area you want to light to estimate a range of light-source lumens. You can also enter the lumens printed on one bulb to estimate a bulb-count range.

Choose your source and target

Each source has its own population, scope and table. Their values are never combined.
Only rows printed in the selected source are offered.

Choose one source. Its room and task rows will appear here.

Enter the area and bulb output

For a room/floor row, enter the room floor area. For a task row such as a countertop or reading area, enter the task zone's area instead. If you use the shared-room handoff, its floor area is copied here and remains editable.

How this estimate works

Required light-source lumens = target Lux × area ÷ coefficient of utilisation (CoU).

The E3 guide describes CoU as usually between 0.3 and 0.7, depending on room size and shape and fitting efficiency. The planner uses both ends: 0.7 for the lower lumen estimate and 0.3 for the upper estimate. It does not choose one hidden coefficient for you.

The selected room/task Lux values remain tied to the source you chose. The E3 CoU range is shown as a separate estimating method reference; it is not a claim that the four target sources use identical lighting standards.

Method source: Australian Government E3 lighting guide, printed page 33.

Important: This is a planning estimate only. It is not a lighting design, not a compliance statement, and not a judgement that a room is adequately lit. Actual illuminance depends on the fitting, light distribution, room geometry and the task's measurement plane. A suitable light meter gives real readings.

Sources and scope

  • UK: Thomas Pocklington Trust, A practical guide to improving lighting in existing homes, 2nd edition, May 2015. A good-practice guide for people affected by sight loss; suggested task and floor illuminances, printed page 22. Open source PDF.
  • Canada: CSA/ASC B652:23, Accessible dwellings, January 2023. National Standard of Canada for accessible dwellings; minimum ambient and task lighting levels, Table 3, printed pages 52–53. Open source PDF.
  • Malta: Building & Construction Authority, Technical Document F, Part 3: Technical Building Systems, effective 1 July 2024. Indicative dwelling light-level guidance referring to CIBSE Guide A, Table 29, printed page 48. Open source PDF.
  • Australia / New Zealand: Australian Government E3, Energy efficient lighting and design: training guide for retailers, September 2014. Joint Australian, State and Territory and New Zealand government guide; residential recommended average illuminance based on AS/NZS 1680, Table 6, printed page 32. CoU method, printed page 33. Open source PDF.

The United States is not supported in this planner because no owner-approved source has been selected. No US room values are inferred from another country's source.
