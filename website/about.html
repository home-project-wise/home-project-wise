ABOUT HOMEPROJECTWISE

Useful ideas for the home you actually live in.

HomeProjectWise is an independent publication of practical, friendly guides for home improvement, organization and smart-home decisions.

Last updated: October 18, 2026

What we publish

Guides for US and UK homes, in four areas: Storage & Organization, Home Design, Weekend Projects and Smart Home. Each guide starts from a real problem, gives a quick answer, then the steps, the common mistakes, what not to buy yet, and the sources behind the advice.

How we work

We explain the useful decisions, show practical steps and acknowledge trade-offs when there is no one-size-fits-all answer. Numbers and limits come from sources we can name, or are labelled clearly as a rule of thumb. Read the full Editorial Policy for how guides are researched, checked and corrected.

Who is behind it

HomeProjectWise is an independent project. Guides are researched and drafted with the help of AI tools, checked against the sources listed on each page, and published under the byline HomeProjectWise Editorial. We say this openly. We do not invent credentials, fictional authors or personal experience, and we do not present a guide as hands-on testing unless it was.

What we are not

We are not a contractor, an electrician, a plumber or a product-testing lab. Brands do not pay to be recommended. Some guides contain affiliate links, which are always disclosed (see the Editorial Policy). For work involving electricity, gas, structure or other hazards, a qualified professional and your local requirements come first.

Keep in touch

Follow new guides through the RSS feed or the newsletter at the bottom of any guide. To report a mistake or suggest a topic, see Contact.

Continuous improvement

We review the site for broken links, weak pages, confusing navigation and repeated content, and we keep making useful information easier to find.
