Editorial Policy

How HomeProjectWise decides what to publish, how guides are researched and checked, and how mistakes are fixed.

Last updated: October 18, 2026

Who publishes this site

HomeProjectWise is an independent publication. Guides appear under the byline “HomeProjectWise Editorial”. We do not invent authors, credentials, tests or personal experience, and we never describe a guide as hands-on testing unless it was.

How guides are made

Guides are researched and drafted with the help of AI tools. Each guide is built around one real problem and checked against its sources, which are linked in the text or in a Sources section at the bottom of the page. Older guides that do not yet have a full Sources list are being updated. Every guide must pass automated checks for structure, images, links and formatting before it is published. The site owner sets the editorial direction and standards. Individual guides are not reviewed one by one by licensed electricians, plumbers, engineers or other tradespeople, so treat them as general information, not professional advice.

Sources and numbers

We prefer official and primary sources: government agencies, standards bodies, manufacturers’ instructions and recognised research. A number, limit or price comes from a source we can name, or is labelled clearly as a rule of thumb or an estimate. When a rule differs by country or local code, we say so and point to the authority to check. When the evidence is mixed, we say that too.

Images and diagrams

Photographs and illustrations may be AI-generated and are labelled “AI-generated image” in the caption. Diagrams marked “Original HomeProjectWise diagram” are made for the guide. Stock photos are credited to their source. Images are illustrative and are not proof that a method was tested.

Recommendations and money

Some guides contain affiliate links through the Awin network. If you buy through one of them, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. A guide with affiliate links says so in a disclosure near the top, and the links are marked as sponsored so search engines can tell. Commissions do not decide what we recommend: we aim to choose products by criteria we state in the guide, and we do not accept payment from brands for placement or favourable coverage. We currently show no display advertising. We name products only when naming them helps the reader decide.

Updates and corrections

Guides carry a publication date and, when we make a meaningful change, a modified date. We do not change dates to look fresh. We review guides periodically for broken links and outdated facts. If you find an error, tell us through the Contact page: we check it against sources, fix confirmed mistakes promptly and update the guide, and for significant corrections we add a short note to the page.

Safety

Work involving electricity, gas, water supply, structure, mould, asbestos or heights can be dangerous. Where a task needs a qualified professional or a permit, the guide says so. If you are unsure, stop and ask a professional.
