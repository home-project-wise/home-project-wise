# HomeProjectWise Analytics & Growth Loop

## What we measure
- Search impressions and clicks
- Organic click-through rate
- Landing pages and returning visitors
- Top content by useful engagement
- Newsletter sign-ups when the newsletter is connected
- Pages with impressions but weak click-through
- Pages with traffic but weak onward discovery

## Operating loop
1. Publish useful content.
2. Validate it before deployment.
3. Watch search and audience signals.
4. Improve titles, introductions, internal links and weak sections.
5. Update pages that have evidence of demand.
6. Use the findings to choose the next topics.

No fake traffic, click farms, automated page-view inflation or misleading engagement tactics.

## Implementation note
Analytics should be connected only after the measurement provider and property are deliberately chosen. Never hard-code invented IDs or credentials into the repository. Until a real property is configured, the site remains functional without analytics.
