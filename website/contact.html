Contact and corrections

Found a mistake, a broken link or a topic we should cover? Here is how to reach us.

Last updated: October 17, 2026

Report a correction or suggest a topic

The fastest way is to open an issue on our public GitHub page. Reports there are public, so please do not include personal information. A short report is enough:

  • The address of the guide.
  • What is wrong or missing.
  • A source that shows the correct information, if you have one.

What happens next

We read every report. Confirmed factual errors are fixed promptly and the guide is updated; significant corrections get a short note on the page. We cannot promise a personal reply to every message, but we do use every useful one. See the Editorial Policy for how corrections work.

Email

A public email address will be listed here when the site moves to its own domain.

Sponsored posts and partnerships

We do not publish sponsored posts or paid placements. Offers of that kind will not receive a reply.
