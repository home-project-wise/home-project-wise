How to make a kitchen counter easier to keep clear

A practical reset for a clearer kitchen workspace.

Start with one prep zone

Protect the area where you actually prepare food.

Give daily items one home

Use one tray for the things that arrive on the counter.

Reset in ten minutes

  1. Clear the prep zone.
  2. Move items elsewhere.
  3. Group daily tools.
  4. Store rarely used appliances.
  5. Wipe and repeat.
