How to Stop Bathroom Towels Smelling Musty

Musty towels are usually a drying and airflow problem, not a sign that you need expensive new towels.

Why the smell keeps returning

A towel that stays damp in folds gives bacteria time to multiply. Bathrooms with weak ventilation make the problem worse.

Change the drying setup

Shake the towel open and hang it across a rail or two hooks so air can reach both sides. Leave space between towels.

Improve the room after showers

Run the extractor fan during the shower and for 15 to 20 minutes afterward. If there is a window, open it briefly when outdoor conditions allow.

Wash for clean fabric

Do not overload the machine. Use the recommended detergent amount, choose a warm cycle suitable for the fabric and remove towels promptly.

Reset a towel that already smells

Wash it separately, dry it completely and check the smell before folding. If the odour remains, repeat the wash rather than adding more fragrance.

Prevent the problem

  • Spread towels after every use.
  • Wash bath towels regularly.
  • Keep the bathroom fan clear of dust.
  • Do not store damp towels in a laundry basket.

When the room itself needs attention

If towels smell musty within hours, check for persistent condensation, a damp wall or poor ventilation. Read how to reduce bathroom condensation.
