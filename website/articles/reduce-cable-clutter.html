Start with the simple check

Look for the actual source of the problem before buying a product or starting a larger project. Small changes are easier to test and reverse.

A practical routine

  1. Identify the exact problem.
  2. Check the simplest likely cause.
  3. Make one safe, reversible improvement.
  4. Test the result.
  5. Only escalate when the evidence points to a larger repair.

Keep the solution simple, accessible and easy to maintain.
