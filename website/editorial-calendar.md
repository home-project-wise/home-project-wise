# HomeProjectWise Editorial System

## Publishing standard
Every post must solve a recognizable reader problem, be written in friendly natural English, contain practical takeaways, and earn attention through usefulness rather than clickbait.

## Daily rhythm
- Morning: one high-utility problem-solving article.
- Evening: one inspiring/practical article, case study, checklist, or smart-home idea.

## Quality gate
Before publishing:
1. Is the reader's problem obvious?
2. Does the article provide a concrete solution?
3. Is the writing human and specific?
4. Are claims cautious and supportable?
5. Is the headline compelling without being misleading?
6. Does the page work on mobile?
7. Are images relevant and properly described?
8. Is there a useful next step?
9. Does it strengthen trust?
10. Would a reader save or share it?
