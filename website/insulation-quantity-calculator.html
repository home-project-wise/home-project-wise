Insulation Quantity Calculator

Measure the surfaces you actually plan to cover. See the area, then optionally count packs using the product name and coverage printed on its label. No framing fraction or pack coverage is assumed.

Measure the area to be covered

Enter each measured rectangular section. If the insulation is fitted between studs or joists, measure the clear area between the framing that the insulation will cover; do not use the full framed wall or floor area as a substitute. You can enter separate clear rectangles for separate cavities.

Optional product pack count

Enter the product name and the coverage stated for one pack. Coverage must use the unit printed by the manufacturer. A pack count appears only when all three details are entered.

How the calculation works

Each entered rectangle is length × width. The optional openings area is subtracted from the manually entered sections. The shared-room wall area, when available, is added separately in square metres. The calculator also shows the equivalent area in square feet.

When you enter all product details, the pack count is the total measured area divided by the entered coverage per pack, rounded up to a whole pack. The calculation does not add waste or an extra allowance.

Important for insulation between framing: the proportion of a wall or floor occupied by studs or joists is not assumed. Measure and enter the clear cavity area that the product will cover. The shared-room handoff represents gross wall surface area after the room's entered openings; it is not a measurement of the clear cavity area between framing.

This tool does not assess insulation type, R-value, thickness, energy savings, vapour barriers, moisture, ventilation, fire, safety, installation suitability or building-code requirements. It does not provide prices, product recommendations or reference coverage. Follow the product label and seek qualified advice where needed.
