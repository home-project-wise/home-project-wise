Baseboard & Skirting Quantity Calculator

Estimate the trim length for one or more rectangular rooms, subtracting the openings where trim will not be installed. Enter the stock length and any extra cutting allowance yourself; nothing is assumed.

Your trim details

Use the sellable length stated by your supplier. The cutting allowance is one total extra length for the whole project, not a percentage. Enter 0 explicitly if you do not want to add any.

Measure each room

For L-shaped rooms, enter the rectangular sections separately. Do not count shared internal edges as trim runs; account for those edges in the total width with no trim.

Room 1

Enter the combined width of doorways or other gaps along the perimeter where this trim will not be fitted. Do not subtract openings where trim will continue.

How the estimate works

For each rectangular room, the perimeter is 2 × (length + width). The entered total width of openings without trim is subtracted from that perimeter. Net room lengths are added, then your entered total cutting allowance is added. The stock-piece equivalent is the total required length divided by the entered stock length, rounded up to a whole piece.

Rooms are measured separately for their perimeter, then their net lengths are combined. The stock count is a length-based estimate, not an optimized cut list. It does not assume that offcuts can be reused or prove that every individual wall segment can be cut from the stated stock pieces.

What to measure

Room length and width

Measure the perimeter of each rectangular room where the same trim will be installed. For non-rectangular rooms, split the boundary into measured straight runs and use a suitable takeoff method rather than treating the room as one rectangle.

Openings without trim

Add the widths of doorways and other perimeter gaps where the selected trim stops. If trim runs across an opening or continues around it, do not subtract that width.

Stock length and cutting allowance

Use the actual stock length available from your supplier. Choose any extra cutting allowance based on your own project; enter zero if you do not want one. The calculator never inserts a waste percentage.

Limits of this calculator

This tool estimates linear quantity only. It does not calculate corner blocks, mitre angles, joint placement, coping, returns, profile-specific lengths, fastening, adhesive, price, availability or installation suitability. It does not create a board-by-board cut plan or reuse offcuts. Check the actual room shape, product instructions and supplier stock lengths before ordering.

Common questions

Does it include extra material for mistakes?

Only if you enter a cutting allowance. There is no hidden waste or breakage percentage.

Can I calculate several rooms?

Yes. Add each rectangular room separately. Their net trim lengths are combined for the stock-length equivalent.

Is the stock-piece count a guaranteed cutting plan?

No. It divides total required length by stock length. Individual wall segments, corner cuts and offcut reuse can change the number of pieces actually needed.
