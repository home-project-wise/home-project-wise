Concrete Slab Volume Calculator

Enter the length, width and thickness you have measured to calculate a volume in cubic feet, cubic yards and cubic metres. The tool does not choose or recommend any dimension.

Your slab or pad measurements

No extra allowance is added unless you enter one. If entered, it is shown separately and included only in the optional adjusted volume and bag estimate.

Optional bag estimate

Only enter this if you have a named product and its published yield per bag. The tool does not look up or assume a bag yield.

The yield must match the named product and the unit shown on its manufacturer information or packaging. The visitor-entered yield is not independently verified. Bag count is rounded up to a whole bag; no bag size, weight or yield is inferred.

Formula and limits

Volume = length × width × thickness. All three dimensions are converted to metres before multiplying. The result is then converted to ft³ and yd³ using the factors shown in the calculation.

This tool gives a volume from the numbers entered. It is not a design, structural recommendation or engineering assessment. It does not advise on thickness, reinforcement, concrete mix, sub-base, curing, slope, frost, loads or code requirements. No waste or uneven-subgrade allowance is assumed. Any extra percentage is entirely visitor-entered and is not a recommendation.

Conversion sources: NIST Handbook 133 (2026), Appendix E, page 245 (volume factors) and NIST Handbook 44 (2024), Appendix C, page C-10 (length factors). The published conversion factors are used.
