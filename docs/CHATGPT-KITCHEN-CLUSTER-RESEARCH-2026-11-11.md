## Primary-source and safety evidence

Official manufacturer instructions show why the article cannot publish universal clearances or suggest operating every appliance inside a cabinet:
- GE Digital Air Fry Toaster Oven getting-started guidance specifies a level, stable surface, 4-inch clearance on all sides and 4 inches above for that model family, and says not to place/store items on top: https://products.geappliances.com/appliance/gea-support-search-content?contentId=000060901
- GE 2- and 4-slice toaster oven placement guidance says to allow steam to escape and recommends 4 inches on all sides: https://products.geappliances.com/appliance/gea-support-search-content?contentId=000060872
- Breville BOV860 manual: stable, level, heat-resistant surface; minimum 4 inches on both sides and 6 inches above; not suitable for a small-appliance enclosure; unplug and cool completely before storing: https://www.breville.com/content/dam/breville/us/en/assets/miscellaneous/instruction-manual/ovens/BOV860-instruction-manual.pdf
- Whirlpool appliance delivery/installation guidance recommends measuring the intended location and delivery path, checking compatible outlets and ventilation requirements, and deferring to the owner's manual for specific installation: https://www.whirlpool.com/blog/kitchen/appliance-delivery-and-installation-guide.html

These examples are model-specific. The eventual article must direct readers to the exact appliance manual, never generalize one manufacturer's clearance to all air fryers, toaster ovens, coffee machines, microwaves or multicookers.
